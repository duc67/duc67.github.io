I am a first-year PhD student in the Computer Science Department at Carnegie Mellon University, advised by Prof. [Tuomas Sandholm](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~sandholm/). My research spans optimization, online learning, and game theory, with applications to AI and Operations Research. 

I obtained my bachelor’s degree in Mathematics  ([Special Program in Mathematics](https://www.math.nus.edu.sg/ug/spm/)) and Data Analytics from National University of Singapore. During my undergrad, I had the opportunity to work with Prof. [Kim-Chuan Toh](https://blog.nus.edu.sg/mattohkc/), Prof. [Volkan Cevher](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/lions/), Prof. [Suvrit Sra](https://mcml.ai/research/groups/sra/), Prof. [Siva Theja Maguluri](https://sites.google.com/site/sivatheja/) and Prof. [David Simchi-Levi](https://slevi1.mit.edu/).

My undergraduate research was recognized with the [INFORMS Undergraduate Operations Research Prize 2024](https://www.informs.org/Recognizing-Excellence/INFORMS-Prizes/Undergraduate-Operations-Research-Prize), Operational Research Society of Singapore Prize 2025, NUS Lijen Industrial Development Medal 2025 (for best NUS undergraduate theses), [NUS Outstanding Undergraduate Researcher Prize 2023](https://www.science.nus.edu.sg/undergraduates/undergraduate-research/outstanding-undergraduate-researcher-prize/).
